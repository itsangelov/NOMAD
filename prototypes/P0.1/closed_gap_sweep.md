# P0.1 Closed-Gap Refinement of the Hinge Feasibility Map (Round A.2b)

**Status: modeled, local only, P0.1 working geometry. No hinge architecture has been chosen, and no "best" point is defined.**

## Two different quantities

- **`LID_CLOSED_GAP`** is the *geometric* standoff between the base top surface and the lid display face when the lid is closed (set physically by bumpers or a hinge stop). It is a design dimension. This refinement varies it.
- **`MIN_GAP`** is the *requirement*: the minimum clearance allowed between modeled bodies in any pose. It stays 1.0 mm here.

In A.2 the two were equal (`LID_CLOSED_GAP` = `MIN_GAP` = 1.0). The closed pose therefore met the requirement with exactly zero margin at every point.

## Method

- Same model as A.2: `CAD/tools/hinge_feasibility_2d.py`, validated against Onshape.
- Same grid: inset −6…10 step 2 × height −4…26 step 2.
- Same sweep: 0–135° in 5° steps, with requirement ≥ 1.0 mm.
- The only change: `LID_CLOSED_GAP` ∈ {1.0, 1.5, 2.0, 2.5, 3.0}. The lid rests that far above the base when closed.
- Regression: with 1.0 the model reproduces the A.2 map exactly (0 differences in 144 rows).
- No Onshape calls were made.
- Raw data: `closed_gap_sweep.csv` (720 rows).

## Observations (modeled)

1. **The closed gap moves the boundary only for high axes.**
   - Largest feasible inset at heights ≥ 20: 0 (1.0) → 4 (1.5) → 6 (2.0) → 8 (2.5) → 8 (3.0).
   - At heights 12–16: 0 → 2 → 4 → 4–6 → 4–6.
   - At heights ≤ 6: 0 at every gap (except inset 2 at height −4 for 2.5 and 3.0).

   In the low-axis region the limit is end-of-travel intrusion at 135°, which does not depend on the closed gap. Values such as −0.59 and −2.00 at inset 2 are identical for every gap.
2. **Margin.**
   - Points with inset ≤ 0 carry the whole closed-gap margin: their minimum is the closed pose itself, so margin = `LID_CLOSED_GAP` − 1.0.
   - Exception: at inset 0 with heights 0–4, end-of-travel geometry caps the clearance at 1.41–2.83. At height 2 it stays 1.41 (margin +0.41) for every gap ≥ 2.0.
3. **Diminishing returns above 2.5 mm.** Going from 2.5 to 3.0 adds points only at inset 8, heights 18–22. The smallest feasible overhang does not improve (52.2 → 52.7 at 105°), because the closed lid also sits 0.5 mm higher.
4. **Cost of a larger closed gap:**
   - About 1 mm more closed height per 1 mm of gap (closed stack 66 → 67 → 68 mm for 1.0 → 2.0 → 3.0).
   - About 0.2–0.5 mm more rear overhang at a given axis point.
   - The display–keyboard closed clearance grows with the gap (2.0 → 3.0 → 4.0 mm).
5. **No ground contact** at any gap. Display–keyboard/trackpad clearance never drops below the closed value. 0.1° sampling changes no pass/fail result (largest difference 0.022 mm).
