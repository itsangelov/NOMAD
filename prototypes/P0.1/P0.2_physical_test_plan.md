# P0.2 — Physical Risk Coupon Package: Test Plan

**Status:** test definition only. Nothing is printed, ordered or tested. No lid thickness, rail architecture, reinforcement, strip material or commercial component is selected. `nomad.yaml` is unchanged.

**Purpose:** move the two largest mechanical uncertainties from analysis to measurement:
1. **Solar rail stiffness:** printed-only vs printed + one replaceable metal strip.
2. **Enclosed-lid rear shell:** sandwich stiffness *and* whether its inner skin actually prints over the core.

**Tools:**
- Existing P0.1 coupons are reused unchanged: `CAD/coupons/P0.1/generate_coupons.py`, and in Onshape the Feature Studio `NOMAD_P01_Coupons`.
- New P0.2 geometry and the expected values: `CAD/tools/generate_p02_coupons.py`, which writes `CAD/coupons/P0.2/{stl,scad}`.

```bash
py CAD/tools/generate_p02_coupons.py
```

To re-predict with calibrated values after testing:

```bash
py CAD/tools/generate_p02_coupons.py --expect --E 1700 --strip steel,1.5,10.0
```

**Evidence levels:**
- Coupon geometry and clearances: *modeled*.
- Expected deflections: *analytical screening* (beam formulas, E = 2000 MPa nominal, **not FEA**).
- Tolerances, bridging behaviour, single-strip load sharing, infill-core shear, print masses and times: *inferred*.
- **The coupons exist to test the analytical model, not to confirm it.** Any result may invalidate it (§13).

**Two problems in the P0.1 rail procedure, found in this review (inferred from the geometry):**
1. **Strip play.**
   - The slot is 10.4 for a 10.0 strip, so the strip is not clamped at the root.
   - Under load the strip first pivots about the pad edge. It only starts carrying load after ~0.4 + 0.4/28.5 × 167.5 ≈ **2.8 mm of tip deflection**, beyond the 1.0 mm display gap.
   - Tested "held with tape" as written, **A2 + strip would behave like A2 empty and give a false fail**.
   - **Correction (procedure only):** shim the strip to a snug fit in Z (§14).
2. **One strip makes the section asymmetric.**
   - D.1 assumed both walls deflect together. With a strip in one wall only, the stiff wall carries the load and the unreinforced wall can lag.
   - Rough bounds for the soft edge at 3 g (tip) are **0.38 mm** (fully coupled) to **2.24 mm** (uncoupled), depending on how the carriage shares load across the floor.
   - **Correction:** measure **both lips** everywhere (§4).

**No new rail geometry is needed.**
- The existing A2 with one strip in its loop-side wall (the +Y wall, away from the ear) is within 1 % of the stiffness of a NOMAD single-strip rail (walls 2.5 / 4.1: EI 30.6e6 vs 30.8e6 with steel).
- A2 also allows the symmetric two-strip state for comparison, at no extra print cost.

## 3. Rail load cases

Loads are static, hung from the carriage by a rod through the post (M5 × 60 or similar) with a bag on each side of the rail. The load mass includes rod + bag + contents and excludes the carriage, which is present in every state. Weigh everything on a kitchen scale (1 g resolution).

| Case | Load | Represents | Apply to |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R0** unloaded | carriage only | geometry, fit, friction, baseline gap | all configurations |
| **R1** static | ladder 100 / 200 / **300 g** | the modeled 0.3 kg cassette (placeholder), 1 g | all |
| **R2** 3 g equivalent | ladder 600 / **900 g** (optional 1.5 / 2.0 kg for A2-1 and A2-2 only, stopping at any whitening or contact) | the D.1 3 g jolt as a **static proof load**. No drops or impacts. | all; A1 / A2-0 may touch the surrogate (record the load at contact) |
| **R3** repeated | 20 cycles (50 recommended) of 0 → 900 g → 0, ~10 s loaded / 10 s unloaded; readings after cycles 1, 10, 20 (50) | settling, strip slip, insert creep, permanent set | the configuration(s) that pass R2; A1 / A2-0 at 300 g instead |
| R-creep (optional) | 300 g for 24 h; read at 0, 1 and 24 h, and 1 h after unloading | PETG creep under the stowed cassette | best-performing configuration |

**Safety:** hang the bag no more than ~10 cm above a padded surface. Never drop or jerk the load. Loads are ≤ 2 kg.

**Known limitation (inferred):** the load is applied at the carriage centre. In NOMAD the cassette hangs off the hub (the pin sits outboard of the carriage) and its outboard edge is not yet supported (D.1 §14). Full cassette weight at the carriage centre is the conservative envelope for rail deflection. It does not test cassette droop or the outboard-edge support, which is undesigned.

## 4. Rail measurements and thresholds

**Stations** (rail X from the outboard end):
- **X 30:** just past the pad (root movement).
- **X 100:** mid-cantilever.
- **X 170:** beside the carriage centre, clear of the load rod.
- **X 196:** the **tip**, the lowest point over the display.

Each station is measured on **both lips**: −Y, the ear / corridor side (the soft wall in A2-1), and +Y, the loop side (the strip wall).

| Quantity | How (basic tools) |
|---|---|
| Rail height / deflection | Digital caliper used as a height gauge: stand it vertically, beam end on the lip top, depth rod to the table; 3 readings, take the mean. Deflection = unloaded − loaded. The table is the reference; the stub must be clamped. |
| Display-clearance minimum | **Feeler gauges** between the rail underside and the surrogate at X 100, 170 and 196, both edges: the largest blade that slides. Minimum clearance = smallest value of all. |
| Permanent set | Re-read all stations 1 min after unloading (and 10 min for R3). |
| Root / insert movement | Height change at X 30 under load. A 0.05 mm feeler at the ear–stub interface under R2 (does it enter?). Before/after, the insert rim height relative to the ear underside (caliper depth rod). A paint-pen witness line across each screw head and the stub: any rotation is visible. |
| Carriage binding | Push the empty carriage over the full 158 mm travel 10× by hand; note catch positions by X. **Tilt test:** raise one end of the board until the carriage slides; μ ≈ tan θ from a phone inclinometer, 3× empty and 3× with 300 g. Repeat after R3. |
| Carriage pitch play | With the carriage stowed, press the post down at one end, then the other. The height change of the post top over its 40 mm length (caliper) divided by 40 = pitch play. |
| Retaining-lip deformation | Slot width at X 50 / 100 / 150 / 190 with the caliper's inside jaws, before and after R2/R3. Optional retention pull: the P0.1 test, 1 kg then 2 kg upside down. |
| Strip movement | Before loading, set the strip end flush at X 0 and mark it. After R2 and R3, measure the protrusion or recession with the depth rod. Check for rattle. For A2-1u, the vertical play at X 0 with feelers. |
| Cracking / delamination | Phone macro photo plus a raking flashlight before and after, at the pad edge (X 28.5, highest bending), the ear–rail junction, the 0.9 mm slot skins (A2) and the lips. Look for stress whitening, layer lines opening, cracks. |

**Optional:** a 0.01 mm dial indicator on any rigid stand, at X 196 on each lip. It materially improves A2-1 / A2-2, where deflections are 0.1–0.4 mm: about ±25 % becomes about ±5 %. Without it, use the load ladder and a straight-line fit of deflection vs load (the slope), not a single reading.

**Thresholds** (deflection toward the display at the **tip, X 196**, on the **worse lip**):

The requirement is the nominal 1.0 mm display clearance (MIN_GAP). D.1 subtracts a 0.35 mm tolerance allowance (RSS of display thickness, seat, pad height, rail straightness). The analytical limits below come straight from D.1 and are **not tightened**.

| Quantity | Analytical limit (pass / fail) | Engineering target | Physical-test warning |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 deflection (0.3 kg) | ≤ **0.50** (δ ≤ gap/2) | the configuration's prediction (table below) | > 0.40 (80 % of the limit), or measured/predicted outside 0.7–1.3 |
| R2 deflection (0.9 kg) | ≤ **0.65** (= 1.0 − 0.35) | prediction | > 0.52, or ratio outside 0.7–1.3 |
| R2 contact with the surrogate | **no contact**; a 0.05 blade passes at X 196, both edges (hard) | — | paper passes but 0.05 doesn't |
| Permanent set | R2 deflection + set after R3 ≤ **0.65**. Set uses the same gap budget; this is derived, not a new limit. | no measurable set (≤ 0.05, the resolution) | set > 0.05 |
| Root movement at X 30 (R2) | none in the model (rigid root) | ≤ 0.05 (P0.1) | > 0.05; **> 30 % of the tip deflection = attachment-dominated** |
| Edge difference at X 196 (R2) | inside the worse-lip limit above | ≤ 0.10 | > 0.20 (asymmetry is real) |
| Slide / friction | no jam over 158 mm, before and after R3 | μ recorded (baseline, no limit yet) | a local catch |
| Lips | slot width change ≤ 0.10 after R2 / R3 / pull (P0.1) | 0 | 0.05–0.10 |
| Strip | no net slip after R3 (≤ 0.5 at the mark) | 0 | rattles, or slip > 0.5 |
| Inserts / screws | no pull-up, no witness-line rotation | — | any movement |
| Cracks, whitening | none | — | whitening = warning; a crack = fail |

**Expected values** (E = 2000; worse lip = the coupled value unless stated; *analytical*):

| Config | R1 at X 100 / 178 / **196** | R2 at X 100 / 178 / **196** | Verdict at the tip |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | 0.19 / 0.67 / **0.79** | 0.58 / 2.00 / **2.36** | fails R1 and R2 (contact ≈ 0.4 kg) |
| A2-0 | 0.18 / 0.63 / **0.75** | 0.55 / 1.90 / **2.24** | fails R1 and R2 |
| A2-1, Al 10 × 2 | 0.05 / 0.19 / **0.22** | 0.16 / 0.57 / **0.67** | R2 **marginal fail** (0.67 vs 0.65) |
| A2-1, steel 10 × 1.5 | 0.03 / 0.11 / **0.13** | 0.09 / 0.32 / **0.38** | meets if the walls act together; the **soft lip could reach up to ~2.2** if they don't (bounds, inferred) |
| A2-1, steel 10 × 2 | 0.02 / 0.08 / **0.10** | 0.07 / 0.25 / **0.30** | as above |
| A2-2, Al 10 × 2 | 0.03 / 0.11 / **0.13** | 0.10 / 0.34 / **0.40** | meets |
| A2-2, steel 10 × 1.5 | 0.02 / 0.06 / **0.07** | 0.05 / 0.18 / **0.21** | meets |
| A2-1u (unshimmed) | ≈ A2-0 until ~2.8 mm tip deflection | | fails (inferred) |

**Correction to D.1, flagged (modeled):** D.1 evaluated rail deflection at the carriage centre. The tip, 18–20 mm further along, deflects about **1.2× more** and is the real minimum-clearance point. At the tip, the single-aluminium-strip variant that D.1 rated "meets, thin margin" (0.92 with tolerance) becomes a **marginal fail** (0.67 + 0.35 = 1.02). The two-strip and steel single-strip conclusions don't change.

**Diagnostic:** for a rigid root, δ(X 100)/δ(X 196) ≈ **0.25**. A higher ratio means root or pad rotation is contributing, which the model does not include.

## 9. Manufacturing test: does the inner skin print over the core?

Run these checks on S0 before loading. Run the section cut **after all load tests**, because it is destructive.

| Check | Method | Acceptable | Warning | Fail |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mass** | kitchen scale vs predicted (S30 ~83 g, S20 ~85 g, INFILL ~87 g at a 20 % core) | within ±5 % | −5 to −10 % (under-extrusion or voids suspected) | < −10 % or > +10 % |
| **Thickness** | caliper at 8 perimeter points + end bands | 6.6 ± 0.15 | ± 0.3 | beyond ± 0.3 |
| **Free flatness (warp)** | largest feeler under the plate on the reference | ≤ 0.3 (the D.1 shell-flatness allowance) | 0.3 – 0.6 | > 0.6. A 301 × 235 shell would warp more (inferred). |
| **Inner-skin surface** (as-printed top = cassette face) | steel straightedge across the width at X 40 / 70 / 115 / 160 / 190; feelers under it | dips ≤ 0.3 over cells; bumps ≤ 0.2 above the web line | dips 0.3 – 0.6 (**sagging**) or bumps 0.2 – 0.3 | dips > 0.6 or visible strand droop (**layer collapse**); bumps > 0.3 (they eat the cassette gap) |
| **Skin continuity (voids)** | phone flashlight behind the plate in a dark room (translucent PETG helps) | even glow per cell, webs visible as straight lines | isolated pinholes < 1 mm | any through-hole, gap between strands, missing skin |
| **Bonding** | tap each cell lightly (pen) and listen; press between webs | uniform dull sound, no click | one buzzing or loose cell | several loose cells, or clicking under finger pressure (**poor bonding**) |
| **Section cut** (after testing) | fine saw across at X ≈ 45 (outside the loaded centre), light sanding, macro photo with a ruler | inner skin at mid-cell ≥ **1.0** (≥ 5 of 6 layers fused); bridge sag ≤ 0.3; no gap at web tops | inner skin 0.8 – 1.0 (the D.1 "effective 0.8" case) | < 0.8, detached strands, or voids at web–skin joints |
| **Dimensional failure** | web lines from the backlight vs §6 (± 0.5); holes fit the frame without force | all | — | webs missing or displaced > 1 mm; holes don't fit |

**B-INFILL specifics** (the slicer, not the geometry, makes the core):
- The top skin prints over sparse infill instead of bridging. The defect to look for is **pillowing** (bumps or holes over the infill pattern) rather than sag; use the same bump and void limits.
- The section cut should show the infill pattern and the number of solid top layers actually printed (expected 6).

## 10. Material and print settings

**Geometry-controlled** (fixed in the STL files; the service must not change them):
- All dimensions.
- Web and cell layout, skin and core thicknesses, cell closure.
- Hole positions.
- The A2 slot envelope.
- **Print orientation:** every STL is already in print orientation (Z = build direction). The rail floor, the stub counterbores, the sandwich outer skin and the B-frame base all go on the bed.

**Printer/slicer-controlled** (not universal; specify what matters and ask the service to report the rest):

| Setting | Required | Why |
|---|---|---|

## 13. Interpreting the results

The analytical model is a hypothesis. Each row says what the result would mean, not what the outcome should be.

**Model validity first** (both coupons):

| Result | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Measured/predicted 0.7–1.3 for A1/A2-0 and B1 | model and nominal E are usable | apply the ratio as E_eff (`--E`) and carry on |
| Ratio outside 0.7–1.3 but consistent across parts | PETG as printed differs from 2000 MPa | re-predict with the measured E; every D.1 thickness shifts (sandwich depth ∝ ~E^-½) |
| Profile shape wrong: rail δ(X100)/δ(X196) > ~0.35, or plate quarter/centre outside 0.5–0.7 | boundary conditions differ from the model (root rotation, joint slip) | **the model structure is invalid for that case**: add root/joint compliance before using D.1 conclusions |
| Ratio differs between parts of the same material | construction effects (bridged skin, strip engagement, asymmetry) | that is exactly what the comparisons below isolate |

**Solar rail:**

| Result | Classification | Consequence for the rail architecture |
|---|---|---|
| A1 or A2-0 pass R1 and R2 at the tip | model far off (predicted to fail by ~3.5×) | printed rail adequate; no strip; rail stays 27.6. Recheck E first. |
| A1 / A2-0 fail; **A2-1 (shimmed) passes on both lips** | stiffness solved by one strip | single outboard strip viable; mechanism unchanged (rail 29.2, loop moves 1.6) |
| A2-1 passes on the strip lip, **fails on the soft lip**; A2-2 passes | **asymmetry**, not stiffness | single strip insufficient → two strips (rail 30.8, **hubs move 1.6 mm**: the D.1 flagged layout change, owner review) |
| A2-1u fails, A2-1 shimmed passes | **engagement/play**, not stiffness | the NOMAD rail needs positive strip engagement (snug slot, shim or clamp). New rail requirement. |
| A2-2 fails, or X 30 moves > 30 % of the tip deflection | **attachment/pad dominates** | pad/ear redesign or through-bolts, or the class B mid-plate module, or more display gap: an architecture question for review |
| Slide jams or μ high | **friction/dimensional** | CLEARANCE (0.3), chamfers, wear strips; check the printed channel width |
| Lip slot widens > 0.1, or retention < 1 kg | **retention** | lip width or thickness, metal wear strips |
| Set after R3 pushes δ + set over 0.65 | **creep/joint** | inserts, screw preload, through-bolts; run R-creep |

**Rear shell / sandwich:**

| Result | Classification | Consequence for the lid architecture |
|---|---|---|
| S30 meets S1 (screwed and unscrewed within the ratio table) and passes §9 | architecture credible as analyzed | class A holds at **~37.6** (lip 1); the D.1 window is unchanged |
| S30 stiffness low (ratio 1.15–1.6), section cut shows good skins | **material stiffness** | class A remains, with T from the ratio table (38.0–39.0) |
| S30 inner skin fails §9 (sag or collapse); S20 passes | **web spacing** | webs @ ≤ 20 (+~11 g, T unchanged); a class A geometry change, not an architecture change |
| S30 and S20 both show a weak inner skin (< 0.8 effective); INFILL passes | **bridging process** | infill-core route (T 37.6–38.0 depending on the measured core shear) |
| Weak inner skin in all three | **skin thickness / process** | thicker inner skin (1.6 → T +~0.4) or the two-piece bonded route; re-test |
| All three fail stiffness **and** manufacturing, or T from the ratio table ≥ ~39 | **architecture** | the sandwich shell no longer beats the modular/ribbed classes; the lid-class decision reopens (class C at 41–42) |
| Warp > 0.6 on the 230 mm coupon | **flatness** | a 301 × 235 shell will be worse: clamping features, a split shell, or a thicker skin; manufacturing/architecture question |
| Screwed ≪ unscrewed deflection | the joint provides real end fixity | usable margin, but the model stays simply supported (conservative) unless the owner accepts joint credit |

## 14. Test procedure

**General:**
- Room temperature. Record ambient temperature and humidity.
- Three readings per point; record the mean.
- Photograph every setup and every anomaly with a ruler in frame.
- One person loads, one reads, where possible.

**A: rail**
1. **Inspect** all parts (§9-style visual check, and whitening/cracks). Weigh each part. Measure the rail's overall dimensions, channel width at X 0, slot width at X 50 / 100 / 150 / 190, floor and lip thickness at the open end.
2. **Unloaded geometry (R0):**
   - Rail floor-down on the flat reference: the largest feeler under the floor along its length (bow) and under each corner (twist).
   - Carriage: 10 full-travel slides by hand (note catches); tilt test μ (3× empty); pitch play.
3. **Assemble:**
   - Heat-set 2 inserts in the ear from below.
   - Screw the rail to the stub (M3 × 8, snug; note the tool used). Draw witness lines on the screw heads.
   - Clamp the stub (X −31.5 to −5).
   - Place the surrogate under X 30–198. Slide the carriage in to the stop.
4. **Baseline clearance:** feeler gap under the rail at X 100 / 170 / 196, both edges (expected 1.0 ± 0.15; if outside, judge by deflection, not the gap). Caliper heights at X 30 / 100 / 170 / 196 on both lips.
5. **Apply load:** the R1 ladder, then the R2 ladder. Hang slowly; wait 30 s before reading.
6. **Measure deflection** at every step: heights at all stations and both lips; the feeler gap at X 196, both edges; the contact check (0.05 blade, paper); a 0.05 feeler at the ear–stub interface under R2.
7. **Unload;** wait 1 min.
8. **Permanent deformation:** re-read all stations.
9. **Repeat:**
   - Configurations in order **A2-0 → A2-1u → A2-1 → A2-2** (then A1 if printed).
   - **Strip fitting for A2-1 / A2-2:** slide the strip into the loop-side (+Y) slot from X 0. Measure the vertical play at X 0 with feelers (A2-1u is tested like this). Then build up the strip's top edge with tape until it slides in snugly with no vertical play. Mark the strip end flush.
   - Then R3 on the configuration(s) that pass R2. After R3: slide test, μ, slot widths, strip mark, insert rims, witness lines.
10. **Document:** photos of the root, ear, slot skins and lips before and after. Record everything in the templates below.

**B: plates**
1. **Inspect:** mass, dimensions, free flatness, inner-skin straightedge survey, backlight, tap test (§9) for S30, S20, INFILL (and B1).
2. **Unloaded geometry (S0):** as step 1. Mark the feeler location at X 115 on the inner skin.
3. **Assemble:**
   - Heat-set 4 inserts in the B frame.
   - Place the 2.0 block at the bay centre.
   - Seat the plate **bed face up**; check both bands seat (0.05 feeler).
   - Screw down (M3 × 12, snug). Place the load bar at X 105–125.
4. **Baseline clearance:** feeler gap to the block at the mark (expected 2.0 ± 0.2). Caliper heights at all stations, both edges, and the bar top.
5. **Apply load:** the S1 ladder (0.5 → 1.82 kg), then S2 (2.04 kg). Place slowly; wait 30 s.
6. **Measure deflection** at every step (block gap, bar top, stations).
7. **Unload;** read after 1 min and 10 min.
8. **Permanent deformation:** centre and quarter points.
9. **Repeat:**
   - S3r (20 cycles).
   - The unscrewed run (screws out, S1 ladder).
   - Optional: S1b (spread bags), S-local (B-L pad over a cell), 1 h creep at 20 N.
   - B1: the load bar plus 100 g and 200 g only, stopping at 5 mm.
10. **Document:** photos under load (raking light for skin waves). Finally the **section cut** at X ≈ 45 on S30, S20 and INFILL: measure the skins, sag and webs, and photograph.

## 17. Summary

- **Reused from P0.1 (unchanged geometry):** A1, A2, pad stub, carriage, display surrogate, load platform, B frame, B1, B-L pad (B2 h 8 optional).
- **New (P0.2):**
  - B-S30 and B-S20 sandwiches: 230 × 90 × 6.6, skins 1.2, core 4.2, webs 2.0, edges 1.2, bridges 27.87 / 20.40.
  - B-INFILL: 230 × 90 × 6.6 envelope, slicer core.
  - 2.0 clearance block: 60 × 60 × 8.0.
  - Load bar: 20 × 90 × 8.
- **Procedure corrections without new geometry:** shim the A2 strips; measure both lips; judge the rail at the tip.
- **Load cases:**
  - Rail: R0 unloaded, R1 0.3 kg, R2 0.9 kg (3 g static), R3 20–50 cycles.
  - Plates: S0 unloaded, S1 1.82 kg (LC1-equivalent), S2 2.04 kg (20 N), S3 recovery + 20 cycles, plus an unscrewed calibration run.
- **Pass/fail:**
  - Rail tip: ≤ 0.50 (R1) and ≤ 0.65 (R2) on the worse lip, no contact, δ + set ≤ 0.65.
  - Plate: ≤ 1.00 at S1, δ + set ≤ 1.00, and §9 manufacturing acceptance.
- **Expected uncertainty (inferred):**
  - Caliper height differences ±0.03–0.05 (3-reading mean); feelers ±0.02–0.05; loads ±1 %.
  - Relative: ~±2 % on A1 / A2-0 R2; ~±15–25 % on A2 + strip R2 without a dial indicator (reduce with the load-ladder slope); ~±5 % on the sandwich S1.
  - PETG as printed: ±30 % expected spread; this is what gets measured.
  - Coupon-to-lid transfer: the rigid stub and screwed frame make the coupons slightly stiff-side. The width-90 strip validates the section per width but not the b_eff = 101 plate assumption. Room temperature only; PETG loses stiffness well before its ~80 °C glass transition, and a sun-heated lid is not covered.
- **What would change the lid architecture:** a sandwich stiffness ratio ≥ ~1.6 (T ≥ 39), or inner-skin failure in all three plates, or warp > 0.6 → the lid-class decision reopens. Anything else stays inside class A (web spacing, skin, core route, T 36.7–39.0).
- **What would change the solar rail architecture:**
  - single strip asymmetric (soft lip fails) → two strips and the 1.6 mm hub shift (owner review);
  - root/pad movement dominant → attachment redesign or the mid-plate module;
  - strips only work shimmed → a positive-engagement requirement;
  - printed rail passes → no strip.
